# Blogs I like

- [Ben Krasnow](https://benkrasnow.blogspot.com/): It's difficult to overstate the impact that Krasnow has had on my life. When I was seven years old, I read about his electron microscope in MAKE: magazine and shortly after saw his acrylic-oxygen hybrid rocket motor on YouTube. He has consistently published great independant science, and in many ways set me on the path I'm on now.
- [Gigabecquerel](https://gigabecquerel.wordpress.com/): Amateur nuclear physics. Although I first heard of the spark plug vacuum feedthrough from Ben Krasnow, Gigabecquerel was the first to publish [plans](https://gigabecquerel.wordpress.com/2019/06/02/quick-n-dirty-vacuum-feedthrough/). Unfortunately, they haven't posted since 2024.
- [tom7.org](tom7.org): I discoverd Tom through his [YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@tom7), and I really appreciate his [computational heresy](https://sigbovik.org/). In many ways, he is my favorite artist, because if developing the most insecure way to get an `https://` padlock isn't art, I don't know what is!
