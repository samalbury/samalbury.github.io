Hello! I'm Sam Albury and this is my site. Currently, (Summer 2026) I am in my senior year studying physics at UMass Amherst. Previously, I attended New College of Florida and Hampshire College, but had to leave both due to [reasons](https://youtu.be/jFMc07F1UUU) [beyond my control](https://www.hampshire.edu/offices/office-president/closure-information). For the past year and a half, I've worked for professors [Andrea Pocar](https://websites.umass.edu/pocar/) and [Krishna Kumar](https://people.umass.edu/kkumar/) on their [nEXO](https://nexo.llnl.gov/nexo-overview) research and development work. Last summer, I primarily worked on improvements to the signal path of Kumar's xenon time projection chamber.This summer, I am heading Pocar's investigation into the reflectance of magnesium fluoride coated aluminum in liquid xenon. In addition, I've been helping develop and document PocarLab's wrapper for the [Chroma](https://github.com/Pocar-Lab/chroma) optical simulation tool.
